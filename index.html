Frequently Asked

Common Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about using and pairing KaifxWasi-Mini (WASI-MD-V7).

What is KaifxWasi-Mini?
KaifxWasi-Mini (WASI-MD-V7) is a lightweight, multi-tenant WhatsApp userbot designed for speed, group moderation, media downloads, AI conversations, anti-delete, and group ring statuses.
How do I pair my WhatsApp account?
Simply enter your phone number with your country code in the Pairing Portal right at the top of this page, click Generate Pairing Code, and enter the 8-digit code inside your WhatsApp Linked Devices menu.
Is KaifxWasi-Mini free to use?
Yes! KaifxWasi-Mini is open-source and free to deploy on Heroku, Koyeb, Render, VPS, or your local machine.
Can multiple users connect to one deployment?
Yes! The bot supports up to 50 concurrent paired WhatsApp accounts on a single Heroku dyno without crashing or exceeding RAM limits.
Where do recovered ViewOnce and Anti-Delete files go?
All single-view media and deleted messages are privately delivered straight to your own connected WhatsApp number's inbox (Message Yourself) with zero public notifications.
